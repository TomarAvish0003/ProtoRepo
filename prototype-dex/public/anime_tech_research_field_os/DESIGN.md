## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-octane anime-tech editorial aesthetic tailored for a next-generation biological analysis console. It breaks away from sterile, empty corporate minimalism by embracing high-density visual storytelling, visceral collector-card tactile details, dynamic mecha cockpit instrumentation, and manga publishing layout energy.

### Core Visual Principles
- **Kinetic Density:** Information is treated as an active readout rather than a passive list. Viewports balance microscopic technical telemetry with oversized typographic proclamations, energetic stat hexagons, and asymmetric panel cuts.
- **Physical Collector Artifacts:** Interfaces merge software readouts with physical collectible card game (CCG) physicality—incorporating holographic foil edge borders, prismatic light shears, chamfered corner registration marks, and stamped holographic foil badges.
- **Manga Editorial Pacing:** Screen architecture mimics serialized tankobon title pages: dramatic diagonal tension, directional screentone patterns, subtle speedline corner motifs, floating onomatopoeia markers (`ドドド`, `ズバッ`), and bilingual diagnostic tags pairing bold geometric roman headers with micro-kanji classification stamps (`属性`, `測定`, `技`, `戦術`).
- **Field-Ready Authority:** The voice balances the hyper-precise diagnostic jargon of a premier biomechanical laboratory with the vibrant enthusiasm of frontline field specimen exploration.

## Colors

The palette operates on a high-contrast warm laboratory base—an off-white eggshell canvas that gives every cybernetic overlay and elemental badge room to radiate maximum luminescence without resorting to a standard generic dark mode.

### Base Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#F6F7FB` (Laboratory Eggshell Tint)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card Bed):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Sub-Panels):** `#EEF2F8`
- **Surface Contrast (Mecha Dark Panels):** `#0D111B` (Midnight Ink)
- **Ink Primary (Headings, Structural Borders):** `#0D111B`
- **Ink Secondary (Body, Field Notes):** `#3A4459`
- **Ink Muted (Telemetry, Micro-Kanji, Axis Lines):** `#7E8BA6`
- **Grid Accent Lines:** `rgba(13, 17, 27, 0.08)`

### Core Cyber Accents
- **Cyber Crimson (`#FF3355`):** Primary action nodes, alert thresholds, capture indicators, S-Rank emblems.
- **Electric Cyan (`#00D2FF`):** Telemetry scanners, biometric shields, target crosshairs, digital sync pulses.
- **Warm Amber (`#FFB800`):** Experience multipliers, warning parameters, physical power stats, gold foil trims.

### Authentic Elemental Type System
Elemental chips, energy gauges, and specimen frames pull directly from calibrated chromatic classifications:
- **Fire (`#FF4422`):** Pure thermal saturation
- **Water (`#3399FF`):** Hydrodynamic azure
- **Grass (`#77CC55`):** Chlorophyll vivid green
- **Electric (`#FFCC00`):** Ionized solar yellow
- **Psychic (`#FF5599`):** Telekinetic magenta
- **Dragon (`#7038F8`):** Mythic ultra-violet
- **Steel (`#B7B7CE`):** Heavy industrial chrome
- **Ghost (`#70559B`):** Spectral twilight violet

### Holographic Special FX Finishes
- **Prismatic Foil Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF3355 0%, #FFB800 25%, #00D2FF 50%, #7038F8 75%, #FF5599 100%)`
- **Holo Sheen Glaze:** `linear-gradient(105deg, transparent 40%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6) 50%, transparent 60%)`

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between hyper-bold sans-serif titles and mechanical monospaced telemetry data.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Editorial Punch:** Hero titles use `Plus Jakarta Sans` ExtraBold (800) with tightened kerning. Where applicable, large section identifiers are paired with a rotated or overlaid translucent Japanese Katakana counter-label (e.g., `MONSTER No. 006` backed by `リザードン`).
- **Telemetry Readouts:** Numerical data, IV/EV percentage scores, and coordinate matrices use `Space Grotesk` with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical scanning alignment across dynamic live readouts.
- **Micro-Kanji Pairing:** Chamfered data badges combine English uppercase labels with micro-kanji pairings in the format `TYPE // 属性` or `ANALYSIS // 測定` at 10px uppercase, anchoring the anime laboratory aesthetic.
- **Manga SFX Typography:** Comic-style onomatopoeia markers (`ドドド`, `ズバッ`, `ピカッ`) sit at a dynamic -6deg to -12deg rotation, styled with high contrast strokes and drop shadows to punctuate stat spikes or shiny captures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an asymmetric 12-column modular rhythm that mimics manga panel compositions. Unlike standard uniform SaaS dashboards, this structure supports uneven, tension-filled card distributions: wide panoramic specimen screens offset by compact multi-tier telemetry sidecars.

### Grid & Viewport Breakpoints
- **Compact (Phone: 360px - 767px):** 4-column fluid system. 16px margins, 16px gutters. Telemetry blocks collapse into stacked battle-ready tactical cards with horizontal-scrolling stat strips.
- **Medium (Tablet: 768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. 24px margins, 20px gutters. Dual-pane specimen profile with floating diagnostic drawers.
- **Expanded (Desktop / Workstation: 1024px - 1600px+):** 12-column hybrid layout. 40px outer margins, 24px gutters. Master-detail panoramic layout with fixed left tactical directory, primary holographic observation deck (7 columns), and right-hand combat telemetry stream (5 columns).

### Panel Construction
- Panels make deliberate use of 45-degree chamfered upper-right or lower-left corner cutouts (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to produce mecha-chassis silhouette contours.
- Card containers incorporate a 1px cybernetic boundary offset with external tick marks at 25%, 50%, and 75% coordinate intervals.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a hard-edged, high-clarity physical elevation model rather than muddy ambient dropshadows. Depth is generated through graphic layering, physical card beveling, and cybernetic luminous glow filters.

### Layering Architecture
1. **Bed Layer (Base Level):** Eggshell tinted canvas patterned with an engineering dot-matrix grid (`rgba(13, 17, 27, 0.05)` spaced at 24px intervals).
2. **Sub-Chassis (Level 1):** Solid white containers with 1.5px hard borders (`#0D111B`) and a 4px offset solid drop-block shadow (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #0D111B`).
3. **Collector Pokemon Layer (Level 2):** Layered card containers with multi-tier borders: an outer 2px `#0D111B` mechanical border, an inner 1px `#00D2FF` cyan hairline rule, and a metallic inner rim bevel (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.8)`).
4. **Holographic Floating Tier (Level 3 - Badges, Modals, Overlays):** Prismatic edge reflection, 6px offset block shadow (`box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px rgba(13, 17, 27, 0.95)`), and neon back-glow (`0 0 16px rgba(255, 51, 85, 0.3)` for crimson highlights or `0 0 16px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.35)` for electric cyan nodes).

### Cybernetic Optical Filters
- **HUD Glass Overlays:** Frosted technical lens overlay using `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with a 3% scanline texture mask to replicate CRT laboratory displays.
- **Foil Stamping:** Foil badges apply an angled CSS metallic sweep on hover that tracks cursor motion across collector frames.

## Shapes

The shape system is crisp, mechanical, and technical (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are tightly controlled at 4px (`0.25rem`) to maintain precision and machine-tooled geometry, avoiding overly bubbly or generic consumer curves.

### Geometric Corner Treatments
- **Standard Corner Radius:** 4px on basic cards, inputs, and list modules.
- **Chamfered Mecha Cut:** Buttons, collector cards, and top-tier headers use angled corner chamfers (8px to 12px 45-degree angle cuts via CSS `clip-path`) rather than rounded corners.
- **Tactical Notch Slits:** Secondary status badges utilize diagonal chevron terminal ends (`polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 8px) 0, 100% 50%, calc(100% - 8px) 100%, 0 100%)`).
- **Speed-Line Accents:** Dynamic accent wedges with acute angles (15-degree slashes) punctuate stat bars and category separation dividers.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Nodes
- **Primary Cyber-Crimson Action Button:** 
  - Chamfered top-right corner (10px cut).
  - Background: `#FF3355`; Text: `#FFFFFF`; Typography: `Space Grotesk` 700 uppercase with `0.06em` tracking.
  - Border: 1.5px solid `#0D111B`. Shadow: 3px 3px 0px `#0D111B`.
  - Icon/Text treatment: Left side features a small blinking square status LED (`#00D2FF`), right side features chevron terminal `//`.
  - Active state: Presses down 2px x 2px with shadow collapsing to 1px.
- **Secondary Diagnostic Button:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`; Text: `#0D111B`; Border: 1.5px solid `#0D111B`.
  - Shadow: 3px 3px 0px `#0D111B`.
  - Hover state: Background switches instantly to `#00D2FF` with inverted text.
- **Ghost Tactical Button:**
  - Transparent base, 1px dashed `#7E8BA6` border, micro-kanji subtitle below main label.

### Pokemon Collector Cards
- **Base Structure:** 
  - Dual-tone panel: Midnight ink upper header band with crisp white specimen viewport.
  - Outer border: 2px solid `#0D111B`. Hard block shadow: `5px 5px 0px #0D111B`.
  - Top header strip displays national dex index (`NO. 0094`) in `Space Grotesk` tabular figures, specimen name in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 800, and a glowing species emblem.
- **Artwork Window:**
  - Surrounded by an authentic physical CCG silver-foil bezel.
  - Background features animated subtle manga speedline vectors or elemental-specific particle fields.
  - Bottom-right corner stamped with shiny capture stars or rarity seal (`S-RANK`, `ILLUSTRATOR RARE`).
- **Data Footer:**
  - 2-column or 3-column micro-grid showing Base Stat gauges (HP, ATK, DEF, SPA, SPD, SPE) with segmented cell fills (like energy bars in tactical games).

### Chips, Badges & Elemental Stamps
- **Elemental Type Chips:**
  - Fully solid authentic color fill (`Fire: #FF4422`, `Water: #3399FF`, etc.).
  - Text: White bold uppercase with Japanese kanji paired beside it (`FIRE // 炎`).
  - Chamfered cut corners with a 1px solid black border.
- **Collector Sticker Badges:**
  - Skewed badge design (`transform: rotate(-3deg)`).
  - High-impact tags: `TOP TIER` (Cyan/Black), `S-RANK` (Gold foil border/Crimson text), `SHINY CAPTURE` (Prismatic rainbow sheen).
  - 1.5px solid white inner border offset inside a 2px dark outer border.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Pokedex Tactical Scanner Input:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`; Border: 2px solid `#0D111B`.
  - Left indicator: Glowing cyan crosshair icon `[+]`.
  - Placeholder: `SEARCH SPECIMEN, ABILITY, OR HABITAT // 検索...` in `#7E8BA6`.
  - Right adornment: Monospace keyboard shortcut keycap `[CMD + K]` rendered as a physical beveled key.
  - Focus state: Border transitions to `#FF3355` with an instant glowing cyan outer ring: `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #FF3355`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Combat Readiness Checkbox:**
  - Square 18px box, 2px solid `#0D111B` border, sharp 2px corners.
  - Unchecked: Pure white interior with faint micro-crosshair `+` center mark.
  - Checked: Solid `#FF3355` fill with a sharp cybernetic check glyph (`✓`) in `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by an animated audio blip visual cue.
- **Radio Selector:**
  - Diamond-shaped outer frame (`transform: rotate(45deg)`).
  - Active state embeds an inner neon cyan diamond pip with a subtle glow.

### Data Lists & Telemetry Feeds
- **Field Note Log Item:**
  - Row layout with high-density data spacing (8px vertical padding).
  - Left-hand status bar indicating research completeness (color-coded by type or completion tier).
  - Alternating light eggshell row backgrounds for scanability.
  - Hover state: Entire row shifts right by 4px with a cyber-crimson pointer arrow `>` emerging on the leading edge.

### Manga Battle Sound Effect Markers (Specialty Component)
- Floating comic onomatopoeia graphic labels (`ドドド`, `ズバッ`, `ゴゴゴ`).
- Rendered in bold red/black outlines, tilted diagonally (`-8deg`), positioned on cards to denote high critical-strike values or special environmental weather effects (e.g., Harsh Sunlight, Sandstorm).
